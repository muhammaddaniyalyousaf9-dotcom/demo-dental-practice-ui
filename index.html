How a camp works

One school day, four stops.

  1. 1

    Set up

    The team arrives early with kits, charts and a screening corner.

  2. 2

    Check every child

    A quick, gentle look with a mirror and a light.

  3. 3

    Brush and learn

    Small groups practise brushing on models, then on their own teeth.

  4. 4

    Send a note home

    Parents get a note, and children who need care are booked in.
